a guild built on one conviction

umain began in stockholm with a simple belief: design and engineering belong in the same room.

not handed off between departments — built together, by people who care about both. that belief became a craft, and the craft became a company. today we are 130+ designers, engineers and product thinkers across four cities, shipping digital products for some of the boldest brands in the world.
