Where we come from

A studio built on one conviction

Umain began in Stockholm with a simple belief: design and engineering belong in the same room. Not handed off between departments — built together, by people who care about both.

That belief became a craft, and the craft became a company. Today we are 130+ designers, engineers and product thinkers across four cities, shipping digital products for some of the boldest brands in the world.
